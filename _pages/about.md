I am **Chenhao Zhou (周晨浩)**, a Ph.D. student in the College of Computer Science and Technology at **Zhejiang University**. My research interests lie in reinforcement learning, representation learning, constrained decision-making, and robust multi-agent learning.

Before joining Zhejiang University in 2022, I completed my undergraduate studies at the School of Mathematics, **Nanjing University of Aeronautics and Astronautics**, from 2018 to 2022. My current work focuses on developing reinforcement learning algorithms with theoretical guarantees for continuous-state decision problems and zero-sum Markov games.

## Publications

**Primal-Dual Representation Learning for Low-Rank Constrained MDPs**  
**Chenhao Zhou**, Chao Zhang, Wensong Bai, Hanbin Zhao, Hui Qian  
*NeurIPS, 2026 (accepted).*  
[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30756) · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.30756)

Primal-dual representation learning for low-rank constrained MDPs with unknown transition features, combining exploration and representation learning with regret and constraint-violation guarantees.

**Toward Online Robust Zero-Sum Markov Games with Function Approximation**  
**Chenhao Zhou**, Jiyu Wei  
*NeurIPS, 2026 (accepted).*  
[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.26286) · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.26286)

Online learning in robust zero-sum Markov games using function approximation, with an optimistic dual robust Bellman framework and regret analysis.

**Solving Zero-Sum Markov Games with Continuous State via Spectral Dynamic Embedding**

*Advances in Neural Information Processing Systems (NeurIPS), 2024.*  
[Paper](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2024/hash/7ed89d8e0b346994b064ba804443689e-Abstract-Conference.html) · [PDF](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2024/file/7ed89d8e0b346994b064ba804443689e-Paper-Conference.pdf)

A spectral dynamic embedding approach to policy optimization in continuous-state zero-sum Markov games, with provable last-iterate convergence to an approximate Nash equilibrium.
